Subject: Request for gravel-bed DEM data (Bertin et al. 2017)

Dear Dr. Bertin,

I am an independent researcher in Japan working on a physically based
rendering project that simulates light patterns (caustics) on natural
riverbeds. To validate my bed-generation model against reality rather
than against synthetic surfaces, I am looking for measured elevation
data of water-worked gravel beds.

Your 2017 paper with Groom and Friedrich reports DEMs of 35 gravel-bed
surfaces, and its data statement notes the data are held at the
University of Auckland and available on request. Would it be possible
to obtain the elevation grid for the "Field 2" surface (and, if
convenient, the Waimakariri surface as well), together with the grain
size distribution metadata?

The data would be used for non-commercial research only, cited in any
resulting work, and I am happy to comply with any conditions you set
and to share the comparison results with you.

Thank you for your time, and for making these measurements.

Best regards,
Yohey Sugahara
