L1. [belongs elsewhere — stage 2 / material transport overlay]
Langmuir cells are real and produce surface convergence, but the primary observable "seams" are material lines (foam, film, debris), not a height-field modulation. Height effects are second-order and weak under the wind speeds and fetches of the target scenes; they do not belong inside the surface-elevation generator.

L2. [MUST-DO in v4-1]
Local-equilibrium gust forcing was already shown to be physically false (relaxation time ~45 s at λ = 0.2 m). A proper wave-action treatment with band-dependent relaxation plus an independent surface-film scalar (Marangoni) is required for any claim that the wind-wave surface is "physics-first." Leaving the broken local-equilibrium model in place violates the governing principle.

L3. [MUST-DO in v4-1]
Bound waves (second-order phase-coupled corrections) and parasitic capillaries are first-order contributors to the high-wavenumber slope and curvature spectrum that drive caustics. Omitting the Sharma–Dean QTF (or equivalent) for the gravity band leaves a systematically incorrect surface that cannot be rescued later by light transport.

L4. [MUST-DO in v4-1]
Uniform-current assumption is an uncontrolled approximation. Real streams have spatially varying U(x,y); the dispersion relation splits into two branches and wave-action conservation must be enforced. Without it the stationary-ring and turbulence components are already wrong in any non-uniform reach.

L5. [belongs elsewhere — stage 2]
Intermittent boils, scars and vortex-core dimples are generated by coherent structures shed from bed obstacles. They are properly part of the bed/obstacle interaction lane (stage 2), not of the free-surface spectral generator. Standing patterns are already captured by the bed-response term.

L6. [belongs elsewhere — stage 3]
Thermal convection cells affect the surface primarily through density and refractive-index variations inside the water column; the height-field perturbation is negligible. Correctly assigned to volumetric stage 3.

L7. [MUST-DO in v4-1]
An "operational prior" that was never validated on the four low-wind cells is an admission that the capillary-floor model is unphysical. Stage 1 cannot be declared complete while a core regime remains formally UNRESOLVED; either close the cells with a physics-based floor or document a hard limitation that excludes those conditions from the stage-1 claim.

Benjamin–Feir / modulational instability requires narrow-banded, weakly damped, nearly unidirectional seas. The project's scenes are broadband, directionally spread and damped; the instability growth rates are negligible. Exclusion is physically justified.
