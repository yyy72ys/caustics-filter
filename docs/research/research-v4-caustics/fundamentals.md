# Caustics fundamentals — added 2026-11-10 (follow-up mission)

- Berry & Upstill 1980 Catastrophe Optics / Berry 1976 Waves and Thom's theorem: in a 2D ray mapping only fold lines meeting at cusp points are structurally stable; higher catastrophes (swallowtail, umbilics) occur only transiently/at points in time-evolving field. Fold dressed by Airy, cusp by Pearcey (diffraction catastrophes, universal up to homogeneous distortion). Sources: GJI seismology review https://0-academic-oup-com.legcat.gov.ns.ca/gji/article-pdf/83/2/477/1613972/83-2-477.pdf ; arXiv 2410.06803 table (fold zeta=2/3, cusp 3/4,1/2).
- Geometric fold: I ∝ |x-xc|^-1/2 on lit side, 0 (geometric) on dark side; wave answer Ai^2: finite, first max at zeta=-1.0188, oscillations inside, exponential leak into shadow; angular/fringe scale ∝ (lambda/a)^{2/3}. https://www.illustrated-physics.com/essays/the-fringes-below-the-rainbow/
- Regularization in practice: finite wavelength OR finite source size (sun disc) — Ezquiaga et al: divergence regularized by either finite source size or wavelength. For water caustics sun-disc + finite pixel/footprint + scattering dominate over diffraction (lambda scale is sub-micron fringe scale (lambda^2 R)^{1/3} ~ microns).
- Ray counting: inside cusp-bounded region 3 rays reach a point, outside 1 (fold crossing changes count by +-2).
- Hannay 1983 / Berry 1977: intensity moments <I^n> ∝ k^{nu_n} with universal twinkling exponents from catastrophe geometry alone; randomness enters only prefactor.
- Dera & Stramski 1986 Oceanologia 23:15-42 https://old.iopan.pl/oceanologia/OC_23/OC_23_15-42.pdf — flash = E>1.5 Emean; freq up to ~200/min at 1m (Veal says >350/min under ideal); freq vs intensity falls exponentially; E>5Emean at most ~1/min; most probable flash duration 10-30 ms at 1m (at 0.5E level), duration PDF skewed ~lognormal; durations increase, intensity decreases with depth; fluctuations timescales fractions of a second to ~20 s; most effective: clear water, clear sky, high sun, light wind 2-5 m/s.
- Gernez, Stramski & Darecki 2011 JGR 116 C00H07 (via abstract mirror): near-surface Ed PDF highly skewed, heavy-tailed; skewness >3 and excess kurtosis >20 at <1m; NO tested model (lognormal, Gumbel, Frechet, log-logistic, Pareto) fits the >90th-percentile tail consistently; lognormal OK within ~20% below 90th percentile for 86 profiles top 10m; PDF -> symmetric/normal by ~10m, skewness/kurtosis -> ~0 at ~10m.
- Hieronymi & Macke: average fluctuation period approx half the dominant surface-wave period (Gernez & Antoine 2009, diffuse case mechanism); flicker called <1 Hz in ecology review but point flashes are 10-300 ms — two timescales: envelope (wave period, seconds) vs flash width (ms, caustic sweeping past a point).
